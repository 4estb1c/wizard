Idle animations

Four ways to keep the hand alive while it waits for you. All four animate transform only, so the compositor runs them and the card faces are never repainted — they cost nothing even with eight cards up. Each card carries its own phase and period so the row never pulses in unison. Hover still overrides everything, so the lift you already have is unaffected; mouse across each hand to check the two do not fight.
