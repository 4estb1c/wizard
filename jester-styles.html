Jester — narrower

Variant 4 throughout — middle 21, prongs 13 — with the whole hat pulled in horizontally. The squeeze is applied to the centrelines before they are swept, not to the finished drawing: squeezing the finished paths would also thin every near-vertical part of each prong, which would quietly undo the weights you just picked. Done this way the prongs keep their 21 and 13 exactly and only the footprint narrows. The brim never squeezes — it stays the Wizard's path — so the hat draws in over a ring that holds still.
