Full deck — monogram ranks, drawn specials

Every rank uses style 1: the rank set in a serif face inside a hairline double frame, with its suit beneath. All five match — the ten simply drops a size to keep the same optical width across two glyphs, and the Ace gives its suit more weight. Wizards and Jesters use style 2, single-weight line drawing, so the four cards that break the suit system also look like they come from somewhere else.
